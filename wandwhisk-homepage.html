Real Ice Cream, Made in Little Rock

You've never tasted ice cream quite like this

So we built something better.

Most shops call their product "soft serve" because it doesn't meet the USDA definition of ice cream. They lean on fillers to keep costs down. We wanted more, so here's what actually goes into ours:

  • ✦
    Real milk cream at 12.5% milk fat. Meets the USDA standard for ice cream, about double what you'll find elsewhere in Arkansas.
  • ✦
    Japan's exacting standards. Quality benchmarks modeled on Japan's luxury soft serve tradition.
  • ✦
    A certified chef on every batch. Swirled into taiyaki cones, potion floats, and seasonal Guild creations.
